Privacy Policy for Inventory Purge

Effective Date: September 21, 2026

Inventory Purge ("the App") is developed to assist Shopify merchants in identifying stagnant stock and automating product status transitions.

Information We Collect

When you install and authorize Inventory Purge via Shopify, we access the following store data via official Shopify Admin APIs:

  • Product details (titles, IDs, inventory quantities, pricing, SKUs, and tags).
  • Order history (timestamps and line item product references used solely to calculate last-sold inactivity windows).

How We Use Merchant Data

Data retrieved from your store is used strictly to display inventory performance within your dashboard, enforce your custom threshold rules, and execute selected archival or status actions. We do not sell, rent, or trade merchant catalog or customer information to third parties.

Data Retention and Security

We retain operational configuration preferences (e.g., inactivity rules, exclusion tags) and local audit logs of purged listings. You can request deletion of all associated data at any time upon app uninstallation.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact support via your developer dashboard.
